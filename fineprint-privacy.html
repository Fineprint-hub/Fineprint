Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information.

1. Who We Are

Fineprint is an AI-powered Terms & Conditions analysis tool operated by Fineprint. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at usefineprint@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Name and email address when you create an account
  • Password (stored securely, never readable by us)
  • Payment information (processed by our payment provider — we never see your full card details)
  • T&C text or URLs you submit for analysis

Information collected automatically:

  • Usage data (number of analyses run, features used)
  • Browser type and device information
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Date and time of access

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve the Fineprint service
  • Manage your account and subscription
  • Process payments and prevent fraud
  • Send important service updates and notifications
  • Enforce our Terms of Service
  • Respond to your support requests

We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties. We do NOT use your submitted T&C documents to train AI models.

4. T&C Documents You Submit

When you paste T&C text or submit a URL for analysis:

  • The content is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) for analysis
  • Free users: submitted content is not stored after analysis
  • Pro users: analysis results are saved to your account history
  • We do not share your submitted content with third parties beyond what is needed to provide the service

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted database provider with industry-standard encryption. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Your password is hashed and never stored in readable form. We cannot access your password.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic: AI analysis provider — receives T&C content for processing
  • Supabase: Database and authentication provider
  • Payment Provider: Processes subscription payments securely
  • Vercel: Website hosting provider

Each of these providers has their own privacy policies governing their use of data.

7. Cookies

Fineprint uses essential cookies to:

  • Keep you logged in to your account
  • Remember your session preferences

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Objection: Object to certain processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at usefineprint@gmail.com.

9. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or financial compliance purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

Fineprint is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or by posting a notice on our website. Your continued use of Fineprint after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at:

usefineprint@gmail.com
